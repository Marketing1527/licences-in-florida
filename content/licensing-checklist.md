# Licensing Florida — Business Checklist

Organized from your notes for clinics and medical locations opening in Florida.

---

## Step 1: First Steps for Any Business

| # | Requirement | Notes |
|---|-------------|-------|
| 1 | **Certificate of Use** | Required for commercial use of your location |
| 2 | **WASD** | (Water and Sewer Department — wastewater capacity verification) |
| 3 | **DERM** | (Department of Environmental Resources Management) |
| 4 | **Miami-Dade Local Business Tax** | (LBT) |
| 5 | **Miami-Dade Certificate of Use (Municipal)** | Municipal zoning and occupancy approval |

---

## Step 2: Medical Office / Health Care Clinic Licensing

| # | Requirement | Description |
|---|-------------|-------------|
| 1 | **AHCA — Health Care Clinic Act** | Standard or Exempt clinic license through AHCA |
| 2 | **Medicare Provider Enrollment** | Reimbursement of Benefits · CMS application (PTAN) |
| 3 | **Medicaid Provider Enrollment** | Florida Medicaid provider application |
| 4 | **CLIA Laboratory Certificate** | Required if your clinic performs lab testing |
| 5 | **DEA Application** | (dea.gov) |
| 6 | **MPI Application** | Medicaid Provider Identification enrollment |
| 7 | **NPI** | (National Provider Identifier) |
| 8 | **D-U-N-S Number** | (Dun & Bradstreet) |
| 9 | **Registry Number Application** | State business and facility registry enrollment |
| 10 | **Sunbiz — Corp & LLC** | (sunbiz.org) Florida Division of Corporations |
| 11 | **Public Notary Apostille** | Notarization and document authentication |
| 12 | **Staffing** | See [staffing page](/staffing.html) |
