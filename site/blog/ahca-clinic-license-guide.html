AHCA clinic license guide

Updated July 2026 · 6 min read

Florida’s Agency for Health Care Administration (AHCA) regulates most health care clinics under the Health Care Clinic Act. Getting the right license type early saves months of rework.

Standard vs Exempt

Standard licenses apply to many clinics based on ownership and services. Exempt status may apply in specific situations — for example certain physician-owned practices. The correct classification depends on your ownership structure and what you offer patients.

What to prepare

  • Sunbiz entity documents and ownership disclosures
  • Proof of location approvals (Certificate of Use, zoning)
  • Medical director and clinical staffing plans
  • Policies, floor plans, and application fees as required

Common delays

Incomplete ownership info, missing local permits, and starting Medicare/Medicaid too late are frequent bottlenecks. We sequence AHCA with DEA, NPI, CLIA, and payer enrollments so nothing sits idle.
