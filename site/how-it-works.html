Process

How it works

A clear path from first conversation to a licensed, staffed Florida clinic.

  1. Tell us your plansShare clinic type, county, timeline, and whether you need licensing, formation, tax, staffing — or all of the above.
  2. Get your custom checklistWe map Step 1 business permits and Step 2 healthcare licenses for your exact location and specialty.
  3. Form your entitySunbiz Corp/LLC, D-U-N-S Number (Dun & Bradstreet), notary, and apostille when needed — so applications go under the correct business name.
  4. Submit applicationsAHCA, Medicare, Medicaid, CLIA, DEA, NPI, and local permits — sequenced to reduce delays.
  5. Staff your clinicMedical directors, physicians, APRNs, RNs, and technicians aligned to your opening date.
  6. Open with confidenceLicensed, compliant, and ready for patients — with ongoing support when renewals come due.
