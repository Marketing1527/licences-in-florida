How to open a clinic in Florida

Updated July 2026 · 8 min read

Opening a medical clinic in Florida takes more than finding a space and hiring staff. You need the right business entity, local permits, and — for most outpatient locations — an AHCA health care clinic license before you can operate.

1. Form your business

Register a corporation or LLC through Sunbiz (Florida Division of Corporations). Many clinic applications require a formed entity, EIN, D-U-N-S Number (Dun & Bradstreet), and ownership documentation. Public notary and apostille services may be needed for certain filings.

2. Secure the location

Before healthcare licenses, your address typically needs:

  • Certificate of Use / occupancy approval
  • WASD (Water and Sewer Department) approval
  • DERM (Department of Environmental Resources Management) where applicable
  • Miami-Dade Business Tax (LBT)
  • Municipal Certificate of Use

3. Apply for AHCA clinic licensing

Most Florida health care clinics need an AHCA license under the Health Care Clinic Act — Standard or Exempt depending on ownership and services. Learn more in our AHCA guide.

4. Enroll for billing & compliance IDs

Plan early for Medicare reimbursement of benefits, Medicaid enrollment, CLIA (if you run labs), DEA, MPI, NPI, D-U-N-S Number (Dun & Bradstreet), and registry numbers. These can take months.

5. Hire the right team

Medical directors, general practitioners, APRNs (including autonomous), RNs, therapy technicians, and phlebotomists — timing hiring with license approval prevents expensive delays.
