Miami-Dade business permits for clinics

Updated July 2026 · 5 min read

If you’re opening a clinic in Miami-Dade County, local business steps often come before AHCA. Missing one municipal approval can stall your entire healthcare licensing timeline.

Key Miami-Dade requirements

  • Certificate of Use — commercial occupancy for medical use
  • WASD — Water and Sewer Department, wastewater capacity verification
  • DERM — Department of Environmental Resources Management
  • Miami-Dade Business Tax (LBT) — county business tax receipt
  • Municipal Certificate of Use — city-level zoning/occupancy

Why order matters

AHCA and CMS applications usually expect a valid business address and entity. Completing Miami-Dade permits first (or in parallel with formation) keeps your clinic schedule realistic.
