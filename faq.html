Most Florida health care clinics require an AHCA license under the Health Care Clinic Act — either Standard or Exempt depending on your ownership structure and services. We help determine which applies to you.
Miami-Dade has additional local requirements including Local Business Tax and municipal Certificate of Use. We include county-specific steps in your personalized checklist.

Timelines vary by license type and county. Business permits may take weeks; AHCA and CMS enrollments can take several months. We help you sequence applications to avoid delays.
Step 1 covers local business permits — Certificate of Use, WASD, DERM, and Miami-Dade Business Tax (LBT). Step 2 covers healthcare-specific licenses like AHCA, Medicare, Medicaid, CLIA, DEA, MPI, and NPI. You typically complete Step 1 before or in parallel with early Step 2 planning.
If your clinic performs laboratory testing on-site — even waived tests in some setups — you may need CLIA certification. We assess your services and advise whether CLIA applies.
